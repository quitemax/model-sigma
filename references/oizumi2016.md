# oizumi2016

**Oizumi, M., Amari, S., Yanagawa, T., Fujii, N., & Tsuchiya, N.** (2016). *Measuring integrated information from the decoding perspective.* PLOS Computational Biology, 12(1), e1004654. Preprint: arXiv:1505.04368.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1004654 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.04368
- Type: journal article
- Open access: yes (summary below is not verbatim)

## Summary (not verbatim)
Proposes a "decoding-based" integrated information: how much information about a system's past
state is lost when the system is partitioned, measured via optimal decoding rather than the
full IIT cause–effect machinery. Tractable on real neural recordings (applied to monkey
electrocorticography across wakefulness/anaesthesia). One of the practical Φ proxies used when
the canonical Φ is uncomputable.

## Role in Model Σ
Cited in §2.1 as an example of how each per-layer Φ_ℓ would actually be estimated in practice
(the canonical Φ is intractable at scale). Relevant to the empirical-instantiation plan. Added
2026-08-28; see `../notes/literature.md`.
