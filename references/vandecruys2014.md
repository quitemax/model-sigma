## Summary (not verbatim)
Deficits in executive function, theory of mind and central coherence in autism can be
understood as consequences of a single core deficit: **a lack of flexibility in processing
violations of expectations**. The mind works by making and testing predictions; in autism,
prediction errors receive a uniform, inflexibly high weight (precision), regardless of context.
The authors propose the theory **HIPPEA** (High, Inflexible Precision of Prediction Errors in
Autism).

## Role in Model Σ
§18 — a closing gap in the model. "The weight given to expectations" is, in
predictive-processing jargon, **precision**, structurally exactly the gate g_ℓ(t). HIPPEA /
aberrant precision is the **context blindness** regime: χ_ℓ(t) = |∂g_ℓ/∂C| ≈ 0 — context does
not modulate the error's weight, so signals that would normally be suppressed keep getting full
weight (the formal counterpart of a "too intense world"). Consequence for Γ: it stays
elevated/variable even in low-novelty environments.
