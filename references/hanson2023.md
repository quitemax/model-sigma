- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1093/nc/niad014

## Summary (not verbatim)
Argues that Φ is **not a well-defined mathematical quantity**: the value it specifies is
non-unique. All published Φ values are selected arbitrarily from a multitude of equally valid
alternatives, and Φ=0 and Φ>0 are often predicted simultaneously for the same system — leaving
"conscious or not" non-decidable in the current formulation. The non-uniqueness arises **as a
direct consequence of the exclusion postulate**, which localizes consciousness by suppressing
intermediate Φ values in favour of an extremum.
