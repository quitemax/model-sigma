# kiebel2008

**Kiebel, S. J., Daunizeau, J., & Friston, K. J.** (2008). *A hierarchy of time-scales and the brain.* PLoS Computational Biology, 4(11), e1000209.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1000209
- Type: journal article
- Open access: yes (summary below is not verbatim)

## Summary (not verbatim)
Argues that cortical anatomy recapitulates a temporal hierarchy in the environment: the lowest
levels represent fast sensory fluctuations, higher levels represent progressively slower
contextual variables under which the faster representations unfold. Cast in a
hierarchical-dynamic-model / predictive-coding framework, with slower states at the top
supplying the priors that constrain faster states below.

## Role in Model Σ
Support for **deriving the layer decomposition from timescale** rather than stipulating it
(§2 / §3.1). Together with `hasson2008`, it grounds "cluster units into bands by characteristic
timescale, then order by predict/error flow." Added 2026-08-28; see
`../notes/layer-decomposition.md`.
