# hasson2008

**Hasson, U., Yang, E., Vallines, I., Heeger, D. J., & Rubin, N.** (2008). *A hierarchy of temporal receptive windows in human cortex.* Journal of Neuroscience, 28(10), 2539–2550.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.5487-07.2008
- Type: journal article
- Open access: no (summary below is not verbatim)

## Summary (not verbatim)
fMRI while observers watched films forward, reversed, or scrambled at different segment
lengths. Early visual areas (V1, MT+) respond reliably regardless of temporal scrambling —
short "temporal receptive windows." Higher areas (STS, precuneus, TPJ) lose response
reliability when long-range temporal structure is disrupted — long temporal receptive windows.
So cortex is organized along a gradient of the timescale over which information is
accumulated.

## Role in Model Σ
Empirical grounding for a **timescale-based layer decomposition** (§2 / §3.1): the brain
already sorts processing into bands by the time window it integrates over. Cited alongside
