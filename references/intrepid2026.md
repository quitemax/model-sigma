## Summary (not verbatim)
The review from the TWCF INTREPID adversarial collaboration. Compares three theories under
joint investigation — **Integrated Information Theory, Neurorepresentationalism, and Active
Inference** — setting out the core claims of each, then contrasting the phenomena they explain,
the kinds of explanation they offer, and their methodological commitments. Concludes that
bridging IIT and predictive-processing accounts is an open, unresolved programme.
