# bayne2016

**Bayne, T., Hohwy, J., & Owen, A. M.** (2016). *Are there levels of consciousness?* Trends in Cognitive Sciences, 20(6), 405–413.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2016.03.009
- Type: journal article (opinion / review)
- Open access: no (summary below is not verbatim)

## Summary (not verbatim)
Argues that the standard "levels of consciousness" picture — a single scale along which global
states (coma, sleep, wakefulness, …) are ordered — is untenable, and should be replaced by a
**multidimensional** account. Global states differ along several dimensions at once, grouped
into content-related dimensions and *functional* dimensions (how contents are gated, their
functional roles). Two systems can be equal on one dimension and far apart on another, so no
single number captures "how conscious" a system is.
