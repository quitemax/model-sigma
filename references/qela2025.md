## Summary (not verbatim)
A PRISMA systematic review of 72 case-control studies of predictive coding in neuropsychiatric
disorders, transdiagnostically. 33 studies covered the schizophrenia spectrum, 33
neurodevelopmental disorders (including autism), 5 mood disorders, the rest neurocognitive
disorders, PTSD and substance use. Result: heterogeneous impairments in patients vs. controls —
the schizophrenia spectrum mainly in non-social predictive coding, neurodevelopmental disorders
more selectively for social cues; the severity of the deficit correlated with symptom
severity.
