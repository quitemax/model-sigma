## Summary (not verbatim)
Proposes IWMT, a synthesis that combines **Integrated Information Theory**, **Global Neuronal
Workspace Theory**, the **Free Energy Principle**, and **active inference** into a single
account. The central claim: consciousness is what it is like to be a system engaged in
integrated world modelling — a self-organizing generative model with the properties IIT and GNW
each emphasize (integration, broadcast) grounded in variational free-energy minimization.
Conceptual and architectural rather than a computed formalism.
