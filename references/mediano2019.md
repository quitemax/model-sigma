# mediano2019

**Mediano, P. A. M., Seth, A. K., & Barrett, A. B.** (2019). *Measuring Integrated Information: Comparison of Candidate Measures in Theory and Simulation.* Entropy, 21(1), 17. Preprint: arXiv:1806.09373.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/e21010017 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.09373
- Type: journal article
- Open access: yes (summary below is not verbatim)

## Summary (not verbatim)
Gives clear descriptions of six distinct candidate measures of integrated information ("Φ") and
compares their behaviour on eight-node networks with Gaussian linear autoregressive dynamics.
Headline result: **a striking diversity of behaviour — no two measures agree consistently
across analyses**, and several take negative values (interpreted as net redundancy). Practical
warning: which "Φ" you pick materially changes the conclusion.

## Role in Model Σ
Cited in §1 — the base measure Σ inherits is not one thing, and candidate Φ's disagree. Also
underpins §7's point that negative integrated information already exists in this literature.
