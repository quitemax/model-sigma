**Mediano, P. A. M., Rosas, F. E., Carhart-Harris, R. L., Seth, A. K., & Barrett, A. B.** (2019). *Beyond integrated information: A taxonomy of information dynamics phenomena.* arXiv:1909.02297 [q-bio.NC]. (Also in *Entropy* `[verify]`.)

## Summary (not verbatim)
Combines Partial Information Decomposition with integrated information to build **Integrated
Information Decomposition (ΦID)**, which splits the whole-minus-parts Φ into components:
redundant, unique, and synergistic transfer plus persistence. Reveals that standard Φ conflates
several distinct phenomena, and that its **negative values correspond to net redundancy** —
the whole carrying *less* than the sum of its parts, not "anti-integration."

## Role in Model Σ
The key reference for §7's distinction: ΦID-style negative Φ means *redundancy* (a measurement
property of a reducible whole), whereas Σ<0 in Model Σ comes from a competing layer's
anti-weight w₋. The novelty claimed is the anti-weight mechanism, not negative integrated
information. Added 2026-08-28; see `../notes/literature.md`.
