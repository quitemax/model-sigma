## Summary (not verbatim)
Shows that the IIT 3.0 definition of Φ presupposes structure (a privileged partition into
discrete elements with well-defined states and a stationary transition probability matrix) that
general physical systems do not have. For continuous or non-Markovian systems the quantity is
ill-defined or arbitrary. A foundational objection: Φ as stated cannot simply be "applied to
any physical system."
